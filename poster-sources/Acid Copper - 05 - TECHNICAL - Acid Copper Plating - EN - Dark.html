Anode Phosphorus Film
Phosphorized copper anodes form a thin black film (containing Cu₃P) that promotes smooth, uniform dissolution and suppresses cuprous (Cu⁺) generation and copper powder. A filmless anode (freshly loaded, or chloride too low) sheds copper particles → roughness. A passivated anode (chloride too high, current too high, or insufficient anode area) stops dissolving → copper depletes and voltage climbs. Condition new anodes and hold chloride in range to keep the film healthy.

Troubleshooting the six most common failures

Roughness / Nodules

Cause: Particulate; anode copper powder; chloride too high. Fix: Improve filtration; check anode bags and phosphorus film; correct chloride to 50–80 ppm.

Burning at High CD

Cause: Low copper; low agitation; current density too high; chloride imbalance. Fix: Add copper; increase agitation; reduce CD; correct chloride.

Dull / Hazy Deposit

Cause: Accelerator low; organic contamination; chloride low. Fix: Add accelerator per CVS; carbon-treat; check and correct chloride.

Poor Leveling

Cause: Leveler depleted; chloride imbalance. Fix: Add leveler per TDS; correct chloride; confirm suppressor is functioning.

Anode Voltage Rising, Copper Dropping

Cause: Anode passivation — chloride too high, low anode area, or current too high. Fix: Correct chloride; add anode area; reduce current.

Blistering / Adhesion Failure

Cause: Immersion deposit — strike skipped or incomplete on active metal. Fix: Repair the strike stage; use hot entry; verify cleaning and activation.

⚠ Safety
Acidic copper sulfate solution — sulfuric acid causes severe burns; copper sulfate is an irritant and highly toxic to aquatic life. Air agitation generates acid / copper-sulfate mist — local exhaust ventilation and mist eliminators / suppressant required. Use PP / PVC / lined tanks — sulfuric acid attacks mild steel. Rectifier = high amperage — LOTO for maintenance. Always add acid to water. Goggles + face shield, chemical (nitrile) gloves, apron, boots; respirator if ventilation inadequate. OSHA PEL sulfuric acid mist: 1 mg/m³ TWA; OSHA PEL copper dusts/mists: 1 mg/m³, copper fume 0.1 mg/m³ (as Cu).
