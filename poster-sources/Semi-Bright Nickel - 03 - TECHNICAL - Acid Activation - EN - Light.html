Dip Time
The acid dissolves surface oxides formed during cleaning and rinsing, producing a chemically active surface for nickel deposition. Under-dip = oxide remains = adhesion failure. Over-dip = etch and hydrogen loading. In a duplex system, adhesion of the semi-bright base layer carries the entire stack — get it right here.

⚠ Safety
Strong mineral acid — severe burns and eye damage. HCl fumes at room temperature (OSHA PEL 5 ppm ceiling). Hydrogen gas evolved during pickling is flammable. HF (if used for die cast) is extremely hazardous — requires a specific first-aid protocol and calcium gluconate gel on hand. Goggles + face shield, acid-resistant gloves, apron, boots, respirator if ventilation is inadequate.
