Troubleshooting 8 common issues

Burning at High CD

pH too low; zinc too low; excess secondary

→ Raise pH; add zinc; check Hull Cell

Dull Deposit

Primary brightener depleted; temp too low; organics

→ Add primary per amp-hr; check temp; carbon treat

Pitting

Low carrier; poor agitation; dirty parts

→ Add carrier; increase air; check cleaning

Haze

Iron contamination; organics; excess carrier

→ Analyze Fe; carbon treat; check carrier; dummy

Dark Streaks / Spots

Cu or Pb contamination; anode sludge

→ Analyze metals; dummy; check anode bags

Roughness

Particulate; Pb; poor filtration

→ Check filter; analyze Pb; bag anodes

Peeling

Excessive stress; brightener imbalance

→ Hull Cell bend test; reduce primary; analyze Cu

Poor Throwing Power

Normal for acid zinc (3:1–5:1); pH too low

→ Optimize pH; accept some variation vs alkaline

⚠ Safety — Acid Chloride Zinc Bath
Acidic chloride solution (pH 4.8–5.8) — causes burns. Rectifier = high amperage — shock/arc-flash risk. Air agitation generates zinc chloride mist — ventilation required. LOTO for all rectifier maintenance. Chloride is corrosive — use PP/PVC tanks, not mild steel.
