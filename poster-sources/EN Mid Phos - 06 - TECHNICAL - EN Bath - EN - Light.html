PHOS BATH

Autocatalytic Nickel-Phosphorus — 550–700 HV As-Plated, 900–1050 HV After Bake

The chemistry deposits the nickel on its own. If stabilizer is depleted, if temperature spikes above 95°C, or if excess catalytic surface is introduced, the bath can decompose — a rapid, exothermic, irreversible reaction.

Operating Temperature — Mid-Phos Runs Hot and Tight
Below 82°C deposition slows dramatically. Above 95°C the bath can decompose. Monitor continuously — a 2°C overshoot matters. Mid-phos runs in a tighter, hotter window than many operators expect.

⚠ Safety — Stage 5
The EN bath runs at 85–91°C and causes serious burns on contact, so wear thermal gloves when handling loads. The reaction continuously releases hydrogen gas (H₂), so keep the tank actively ventilated and free of ignition sources. Dissolved nickel is a skin sensitizer (GHS Cat. 1) and a known carcinogen (IARC Group 1, GHS Category 1A), so avoid repeated skin contact and wear nitrile gloves. If the bath begins to decompose, evacuate immediately and do not try to control it: it can release phosphine gas (PH₃, TLV 0.05 ppm) and boil over.
