Electroplating — Zinc Plate (Acid)
ZINC PLATE (ACID)
Acid chloride or sulfate zinc electrodeposition
Acid zinc plating delivers bright, smooth deposits at higher plating speeds than alkaline zinc, commonly used on fasteners and stampings.

THE KEY NUMBER
pH 4.8–5.4
Bath pH directly controls deposit brightness and current efficiency — out-of-range pH causes dull, rough, or burned deposits.
WHAT YOU MUST DO
1
Check pH at the start of every shift and adjust with hydrochloric acid (to lower) or potassium hydroxide (to raise)
2
Maintain bath temperature between 70–95 °F (21–35 °C) — higher temperatures require more brightener
3
Operate at 20–50 ASF (2.2–5.4 A/dm²) for rack work and up to 80 ASF (8.6 A/dm²) for barrel work
4
Run continuous filtration through carbon cartridges to keep organics under control
5
Perform Hull cell analysis every 4 hours during production to monitor brightener consumption and replenish as needed
COMMON PROBLEMS
Problem What to Look For What to Do
Rough or gritty depositParts feel rough to the touch like sandpaper, especially on flat surfacesCheck filtration — may be clogged; dummy plate at 5 ASF for 2–4 hours to remove metallic contaminants; verify anode bags are intact
White haze or dullnessParts lack brightness and have a white, cloudy appearance even on high-current areasRun Hull cell — add primary brightener if low-current area is dull first; carbon-treat if brightener addition does not resolve
Skip platingBare spots or areas with no zinc coverage, often in recesses or near maskingCheck cleaning line — parts may have residual oils; verify pH is not too low (below 4.5 causes passivation of steel substrate)
⚠ SAFETY
●Bath contains hydrochloric acid — corrosive fumes attack skin, eyes, and lungs; ensure tank-side ventilation is running
●Wear acid-rated splash goggles, face shield, and PVC or rubber gloves when handling bath chemicals
●Do not mix acid zinc chemicals with alkaline products — violent reactions and toxic gas can result
●Rinse splashes off skin immediately with water for at least 15 minutes; seek medical attention for burns larger than your palm
Consult the supervisor before adjusting process parameters.
