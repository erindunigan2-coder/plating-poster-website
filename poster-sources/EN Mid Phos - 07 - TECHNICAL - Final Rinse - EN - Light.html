Mid-Phos Surface Sensitivity
Mid-phos produces a smoother, semi-bright surface compared to matte low-phos. This means fingerprints, contact marks, and water spots are more visible. Handle parts with clean nitrile gloves at all times. Never stack parts while wet — contact marks show through the final appearance. Keep parts wet until they enter the oven or final treatment.

⚠ Safety — Final Rinse
Rinse water contains dissolved nickel — a regulated heavy metal. Goes to treatment system, never to direct drain. Nickel is a sensitizer — avoid repeated skin contact; use nitrile gloves. Wet floors are a slip hazard. Report any nickel solution spill immediately.
