Key Rules nickel system

1Semi-bright Ni must be ≥60% of total Ni thickness — this ratio drives corrosion protection
2STEP test ΔE should be 100–200 mV between semi-bright and bright — below 100 mV, sacrificial protection fails
3Semi-bright Ni must contain zero sulfur — any sulfur destroys the potential difference
4Microporous Ni spreads corrosion into >10,000 tiny cells instead of deep pits
5Control pH and boric acid — low pH burns edges, low boric acid causes pitting
⚠ Safety
Nickel salts are a skin sensitizer (GHS Cat. 1), and soluble nickel compounds are a Category 1A carcinogen by inhalation (IARC Group 1, a known human carcinogen), so minimize aerosol. Boric acid is a reproductive toxin at high exposure. Gloves and local exhaust are mandatory, and an emergency shower and eyewash must be within 10 seconds of the station.
