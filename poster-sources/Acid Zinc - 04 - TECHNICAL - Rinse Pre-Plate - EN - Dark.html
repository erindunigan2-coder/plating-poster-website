Excess acid drag-in depresses plating bath pH, accelerates brightener breakdown, and introduces dissolved iron from the pickle tank. Spent pickle = liquid contamination. This rinse is your filter.

Key Procedures 6 steps

1
Drain 15–20 sec over pickle tank before transferring. Every second of drain saves acid from entering this rinse.
2
Immerse fully with agitation. Rack movement or air sparging for 30–60 sec minimum.
3
Use double rinse (counter-current) when possible — reduces drag-in to plating bath by 90%+.
4
Check conductivity every shift. Log readings. Action limit: >200 µS/cm. Dump limit: >500 µS/cm.
5
Drain 10–15 sec over rinse tank before entering plating bath. Minimize volume drag-in.
6
Transfer to plating immediately. Activated steel re-oxidizes within seconds in air.
Iron Drag-In — Why This Rinse Matters More in Acid Zinc

Unlike alkaline zinc (where iron precipitates as hydroxide and is filtered), iron drag-in to an acid zinc bath stays in solution and accumulates. The pre-plate rinse is your primary defense against iron contamination from the pickle.

Do best practice

  • Use DI or RO water to minimize chloride and mineral content
  • Log conductivity readings every shift — trend it weekly
  • Maintain continuous overflow at 3–5 gal/min minimum
  • Agitate parts during rinse — still water doesn’t rinse

Don’t avoid

  • Skip conductivity checks — you won’t see iron until it’s in the bath
  • Let parts dry between rinse and plate — instant re-oxidation
  • Use tap water with high TDS — defeats the purpose of guarding
  • Run stagnant rinse — dead water accumulates contaminants fast

Troubleshooting Common issues

Conductivity High

Heavy acid drag-out; low flow

Increase drain time from pickle; increase flow rate.

Plating Bath pH Dropping Fast

Acid drag-in from pickle

Tighten conductivity limit; add double rinse stage.

Iron Climbing in Plating Bath

Dissolved Fe drag-in from spent pickle

Dump/refresh pickle; improve rinse; add second stage.

Parts Oxidizing Before Plating

Transfer too slow; drying

Speed up — enter plating within 30 sec of leaving rinse.

⚠ Safety
Dilute acid residue. Chemical splash goggles, chemical-resistant gloves, non-slip footwear. Eye contact: flush at eyewash 15 min.
