⚠ Iron Drag-In — Why This Rinse Matters More in Acid Zinc

Unlike alkaline zinc (where iron precipitates out and gets filtered), iron drag-in to an acid zinc bath stays dissolved and accumulates. Spent pickle = liquid contamination. This rinse is your primary defense.

⚠ Safety
Dilute acid residue in the water. Wear chemical splash goggles and chemical-resistant gloves. Non-slip footwear required. Eye contact: flush at eyewash 15 min.
