Final Barrier — Enhanced Corrosion + Friction Control

Sealers close microscopic pores in the passivation film and control torque-tension coefficient. On acid Zn-Ni’s brighter substrate, sealer appearance is often more uniform.

Coefficient of Friction — Torque-Tension for Fasteners
Automotive OEMs specify K = 0.12–0.18. Acid Zn-Ni’s brighter, smoother surface may produce slightly lower baseline friction — verify sealer concentration accordingly. The sealer adds an organic or inorganic topcoat over the passivation layer, significantly boosting salt spray performance. It fills micro-cracks in the passivation film.

Key Procedures 7 steps

1
Verify sealer concentration by refractometer — typically 5–15% v/v. Brighter acid Zn-Ni substrate may need higher concentration for target K-factor.
2
Verify temp 20–35°C (some silicate types require 40–50°C). Check supplier TDS.
3
Immerse passivated parts 30–60 s with gentle agitation. Ensure full coverage on all surfaces.
4
Withdraw slowly 2–5 cm/s for uniform coverage. Drip marks are more visible on bright acid Zn-Ni — control speed carefully.
5
Dry at 80–120°C for 10–20 min. Silicate: 80°C/15 min. Silane: 100–120°C/10–15 min. Organic polymer: 80–100°C/10–20 min.
6
Test friction coefficient on 3–5 parts per rack. Record K-factor. Reject rack if out of 0.12–0.18 range.
7
Visual inspect for drip marks, pooling, bare spots. Bright substrate makes defects more conspicuous — check under strong lighting.

Do best

  • Verify concentration every shift by refractometer
  • Test friction on 3–5 parts per rack
  • Control withdrawal speed for uniform film
  • Keep oven temperature <150°C to protect passivation
  • Verify complete and uniform sealer coverage before racking for cure

Don’t avoid

  • Mix different sealer chemistries in the same tank
  • Apply sealer to wet/dripping parts
  • Skip sealer for automotive OEM work
  • Re-dip dried parts — double film causes peeling
  • Stack sealed parts before cure is complete — sealer transfers and creates bare spots

Troubleshooting

Friction Out of Spec

Concentration drift or uneven drying. Bright acid Zn-Ni may need higher sealer concentration for same K-factor. Verify oven temp uniformity.

Drip Marks / Pooling

Withdrawal too fast or parts not oriented for drainage. Drip marks more visible on bright acid Zn-Ni — slow withdrawal to 2–3 cm/s.

Sealer Peeling / Flaking

Applied too thick (double dip or high concentration), or over-baked. Reduce concentration; check oven temp <150°C.

Cloudy / Hazy Appearance

Hard water in sealer or contamination. Check DI water quality; filter sealer tank; check for cross-contamination from acid bath drag-in.

Acid Zn-Ni Note
Brighter substrate affects sealer wetting — may need higher concentration for same K-factor. Drip marks more visible on bright acid Zn-Ni. Inspect under strong lighting and adjust withdrawal speed as needed.
⚠ Safety — Sealers & Ovens
Most sealers are mildly acidic to neutral — wear gloves and splash goggles as standard PPE. Some silane-based sealers are flammable — check SDS for flash point and keep away from ignition sources. Drying ovens at 80–120°C present burn risk — heat-resistant gloves required when loading/unloading. Vent oven exhaust to prevent VOC accumulation. Sealer solutions may contain organic solvents. Ensure adequate ventilation. Avoid prolonged skin contact and wear chemical-resistant gloves.
