Step by Step — Full Sequence
Process Temp Time Key Parameter
Alkaline Clean (AL-SAFE only) 130–150°F 3–5 min If it etches, WRONG cleaner — stop
DI Rinse Ambient 30–60 s Flowing water. Don’t let parts dry.
Acid Etch / Deoxidize 70–100°F 15–60 s Bright aluminum. No dark smut.
DI Rinse Ambient 30–60 s Transfer immediately — NO air dry
First Zincate 65–80°F 30–60 s Uniform gray. No bare spots.
DI Rinse Ambient 30–60 s Quick — keep parts wet
Zincate Strip (Nitric) Ambient 10–15 s All zinc gone. Bare bright aluminum.
DI Rinse Ambient 30–60 s Transfer immediately to the second zincate
Second Zincate 65–80°F 15–30 s Thinner, lighter gray than the first zincate
DI Rinse Ambient 30–60 s Go straight to Cu strike — no delay
COPPER STRIKE (cyanide) 90–110°F 1–3 min Current ON before parts enter
Transfer Rinse Ambient 30–60 s Quick rinse between strike and plate
COPPER PLATE (cyanide) 140–160°F Per supervisor Time depends on thickness target
Rinse (multi-stage) Ambient 60 s/stage First tank is recovery — don’t dump
SILVER STRIKE (cyanide) 70–80°F 30–90 s LIVE ENTRY — current ON before parts enter
SILVER PLATE (cyanide) 70–90°F Per supervisor Check thickness with XRF
DI Rinse (multi-stage) Ambient 30–60 s ea First tank is Ag recovery — valuable
Anti-Tarnish (if spec allows) Amb–104°F 15–30 s Skip if spec prohibits coatings
Final Rinse and Dry 104–131°F Until dry Clean gloves. No bare hands.
Watch Out — These Five Things Cause 90% of Rejects
1
PARTS DRYING BETWEEN STEPS — Oxide re-forms in seconds. Keep wet between etch and Cu strike.
2
SKIPPING THE DOUBLE ZINCATE — Single zincate = blisters in service. Always strip and re-zincate.
3
ENTERING CYANIDE BATHS WITHOUT CURRENT — Copper strike & silver strike: rectifier ON before parts go in.
4
ACID IN CYANIDE TANKS — Acid + cyanide = HCN gas = lethal. Rinse thoroughly between zones.
5
IGNORING BATH CHANGES — Color, foam, smell different? Stop. Tag the tank. Call your supervisor.
⚠ Safety — Cyanide Line
CYANIDE KILLS. This line has FOUR cyanide baths — the copper strike, copper plate, silver strike, and silver plate. No eating, drinking, or smoking near the line. NEVER add acid to cyanide — HCN gas is lethal in minutes. Wear face shield, gloves, apron at every bath. Ventilation must be running. Know where the antidote kit is. If the HCN alarm sounds, leave immediately.
