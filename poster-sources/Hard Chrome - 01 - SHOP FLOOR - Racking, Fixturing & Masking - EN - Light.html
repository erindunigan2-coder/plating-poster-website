✓ Do
  • Clean contacts to bare metal before every use
  • Mask all non-plate surfaces completely
  • Install conforming anodes for bores and recesses
  • Verify contact resistance with meter before plating
✗ Don’t
  • Use corroded or chrome-coated contacts
  • Leave gaps or pinholes in masking
  • Assume the main anode will reach deep cavities
  • Start plating without confirming thickness requirements
⚙ Operator Tips
Pre-plate checklist: Verify anode-to-cathode spacing matches the job traveler. Check that all masking is sealed at edges — chromic acid will wick under loose tape. For ID chrome, confirm the conforming anode is centered and supported to prevent contact with the bore wall. Log contact resistance readings on the batch record.
⚠ Safety — Stage 1
Heavy parts and sharp fixtures — cut-resistant gloves (ANSI A4+), mechanical assists for parts >35 lbs. Lead tape and lead anodes — OSHA PEL 50 µg/m³; nitrile gloves, no eating/drinking in work area, dispose as hazardous waste. Solvent-based lacquers — apply in ventilated area with organic-vapor respirator. When working near active chrome tank, full Cr(VI) PPE required.
Operator reference only. Follow your facility SOPs. Report any process deviations to your supervisor.
