⚠ Hydrogen Embrittlement — Bake Requirement
For hardened steel (>40 HRC): bake within 4 hours of last electroplating step at 375 ±25°F for minimum 8 hours per ASTM B850. Some specifications require baking before passivation.

Troubleshooting 3 common issues

Passivate Bath Life Short

Chloride drag-in from acid zinc bath. Tighten rinse conductivity; add second rinse stage.

Blotchy Passivate

Residual chloride on zinc deposit. Improve rinse agitation and increase immersion time.

White Haze on Zinc

Zinc oxidation or hard water deposits. Use DI water; reduce transfer time between tanks.
