Water Break Time — That’s the Target
Pull the part out and hold it vertical. Water should sheet off in a continuous film. If water beads up anywhere, the part is NOT clean. Re-clean it.
Water Break Test

What You Must Do
1
Check the tank temperature. Should read 130–160°F. If it’s cold, the cleaner won’t work.
2
Submerge parts fully. All surfaces must be wetted — trapped air pockets prevent cleaning.
3
Soak 3–10 min. Heavy oil needs the full soak. Light stamping oil: 3–5 min. Keep parts moving.
4
Water break test every load. Dip in clean water. Water must sheet uniformly. Any beading = re-clean.
5
Transfer to rinse immediately. Do not let the cleaner dry on the part.

⚠ Safety
Hot alkaline solution — chemical burns on contact. Wear face shield, chemical gloves (elbow-length), and apron. Tank temperature (130–160°F) is hot enough to scald — lower parts slowly, do not splash. Skin contact: flush 15 min. Eyes: eyewash 15 min.
