Weight Savings vs. Copper Busbars
Aluminum busbars at one-third the density of copper. Silver contact surfaces at 106% IACS. Copper interlayer for adhesion and conductivity buildup. The best of three metals in one part.

Why This Multi-Layer Approach Tri-metal logic

Rectifiers distribute high-amperage current to electrochemical process lines. Every micro-ohm of contact resistance converts current to waste heat. This tri-layer system is standard in switchgear, battery interconnects, EV power distribution, and electrochemical rectifier installations.

⚠ Cyanide Safety — The Cyanide Zone
FOUR CYANIDE BATHS in this zone — the Cu cyanide strike, the Cu cyanide plate, the Ag cyanide strike, and the Ag cyanide plate. (The transfer and multi-stage rinses between them are water only.) KCN and NaCN are lethal by ingestion, inhalation, and skin absorption. NEVER mix cyanide with acid — HCN gas is rapidly fatal; 50 ppm is the IDLH (Immediately Dangerous to Life or Health) level. Continuous HCN monitors required. Emergency antidote kits must be on-site.
