Cascade Rinse + Contamination Threats
THREAT 1: CHLORIDE
From HCl activation. Cl⁻ >5 ppm causes pitting in EN bath. DI rinse essential.
KILL THRESHOLD: Cl⁻ >5 ppm = pitting
THREAT 2: CHROMATE
From plastic etch process. Poisons EN bath at trace ppm levels. Cr6+ = bath death.
KILL THRESHOLD: Cr⁶⁺ >0.5 ppm = bath inert
THREAT 3: ZN OXIDATION
Zincated aluminum surface. Transfer to EN bath within 30 sec. Oxidized Zn = no initiation.
TIME LIMIT: <30 sec rinse-to-EN

CRITICAL TIMING — ZINCATED ALUMINUM
The zincate layer oxidizes immediately on leaving the bath. Oxidized zinc will not initiate the EN reaction.
0–30 sec: OK WINDOW
RISK ZONE — Zn oxidizes >30 sec
● RULE: Transfer from pre-plate rinse to EN bath within 30 seconds
● Never allow zincated parts to air-dry between stations
● Stage tanks to minimize travel time — physical layout matters
Safety & Waste
● Rinse water is mildly acidic — pH 4–7 from activator drag-out
● Chromate rinse water = Cr6+ hazardous waste — dedicated sump, permitted treatment
● Acid rinse overflow to waste treatment — not direct drain
● Wet floor hazard — non-slip matting required
● Check pH of overflow periodically
● Nitrile gloves and splash goggles at all rinse stations
Technical reference only. Values are typical operating ranges — consult chemistry supplier for bath-specific parameters. ASTM B733 governs EN plating specifications.
