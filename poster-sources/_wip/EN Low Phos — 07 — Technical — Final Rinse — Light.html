Post-Plate Rinse — Stop the Reaction, Protect the Deposit
⚠ NO AIR-DRY
Transfer from bath to rinse immediately. EN solution dried on surface = permanent stains and uneven finish that cannot be removed.
⚠ NO FINGER CONTACT
Fresh EN deposit is sensitive. Fingerprints etch into the surface and become visible after drying or heat treatment. Gloves at all times.
⚠ NO WET STACKING
Do not stack parts while wet. Galvanic attack occurs at metal-to-metal contact points during wet storage. Parts must dry individually.

The Three Rules
1. NO AIR-DRY
Transfer bath → rinse in <10 seconds. EN solution dries as a permanent stain.
2. NO FINGER CONTACT
Fresh EN is soft. Fingerprints etch permanently. Nitrile gloves mandatory post-bath.
3. NO WET STACKING
Metal contact while wet causes galvanic pitting at contact points. Rack individually.

Drag-Out Recovery
● Drag-out contains dissolved Ni²⁺ + hypophosphite
● Static drag-out tank recovers 30–50% of value
● Recovered solution can be returned to EN bath
● Reduces waste treatment load — valuable chemistry
● Check Ni and P content of recovery tank weekly
Safety & Waste
● Nickel is a regulated heavy metal — no untreated discharge
● Rinse overflow pH 8–9 — route to waste treatment
● Discharge permit required in most jurisdictions
● Wet floor hazard — non-slip matting required
● SDS for EN bath chemistry posted at line
