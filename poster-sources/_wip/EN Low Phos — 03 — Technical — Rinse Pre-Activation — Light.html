Why This Rinse Matters Failure Modes

Key Risks if Rinse Fails

1Alkaline drag-in neutralizes HCl or H₂SO₄ activator — activation fails silently.
2On aluminum: alkaline carry-over re-dissolves the fresh zincate layer before activation.
3Drag-out rate ~0.5–2.0 gal / 1000 ft² — multiply by part area to size cascade tanks.
Efficiency Note
Counter-current cascade: tank 3 overflows into tank 2, tank 2 overflows into tank 1. Drain tank 1 for waste treatment. Most economical design for water use and chemical recovery.

Safety Hazards & PPE
  • Rinse water is mildly alkaline — pH 9–11
  • Overflow to waste treatment — never discharge untreated
  • Wet floors create slip hazard — non-slip matting required
  • Check rinse pH periodically — alkaline carry-over raises pH
  • Nitrile gloves and splash goggles during tank maintenance
