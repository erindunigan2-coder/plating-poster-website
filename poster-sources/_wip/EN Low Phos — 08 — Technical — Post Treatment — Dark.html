Hydrogen Embrittlement Relief — The 4-Hour Window
PLATE DONE
0–4 hr: BAKE WINDOW
>4 hr: RISK ZONE
High-strength steel (>1000 MPa UTS or >40 HRC):
● Bake at 190–210°C within 4 hours of plating completion
● Minimum hold: 4 hours (ASTM B849)
● Up to 23 hours for critical aerospace applications (ASTM B850)
● FAILURE TO BAKE = RISK OF DELAYED BRITTLE FRACTURE
Refs: ASTM B849 / B850 / AMS 2759/9

Furnace Safety
● Furnace at 190–400°C — severe burn hazard
● Heat-resistant gloves required for loading
● Ventilation required — H₂ released during bake
● Inert atmosphere (N₂, Ar) — asphyxiation risk — verify O₂ before entry
● Allow parts to cool before handling
● Calibration: ±5°C across load; thermocouples annual
● Log every cycle: part ID, temp, time, furnace ID
