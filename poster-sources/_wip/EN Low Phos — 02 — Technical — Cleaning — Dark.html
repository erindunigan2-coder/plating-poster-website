Safety Hazards & PPE

Chemical Hazards

  • NaOH — severe skin/eye burns on contact
  • Hot solution (60–80°C) — thermal burn risk
  • Mist and vapor — irritates respiratory tract
  • NEVER add water to NaOH concentrate — add caustic to water
  • H₂ gas evolved during electrocleaning — ventilate

PPE Required

  • Chemical splash goggles (not safety glasses)
  • Chemical-resistant gloves — nitrile or neoprene
  • Chemical-resistant apron or lab coat
  • Eyewash station within 10 seconds of tank
  • SDS posted at point of use and in safety binder
  • Overhead ventilation at tank line

Technical reference only. Values shown are typical operating ranges — consult your chemistry supplier for bath-specific parameters. ASTM B733 governs EN plating specifications.
