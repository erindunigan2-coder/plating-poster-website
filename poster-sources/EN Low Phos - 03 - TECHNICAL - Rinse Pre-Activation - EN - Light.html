Remove Alkaline Drag-Out Before Acid Activation

If you drag alkali into the activator, the acid is neutralized, activation fails, and the part enters the EN bath without proper surface preparation — no visible warning, just a bad deposit.

Between 50 and 500 µS/cm — increase water flow and re-check. Above 500 µS/cm — dump the tank and refill. Conductivity is your early-warning system for drag-out contamination.

Drag-Out Economics
Alkaline cleaner drag-out neutralizes the acid activator downstream. Every gram of NaOH carried over consumes acid, raises activation pH, and weakens the oxide dissolution. Two-stage counter-flow rinsing achieves ~900:1 dilution ratio at 70–85% lower water consumption than single-overflow rinsing.

⚠ Safety — Stage 2
Residual alkaline cleaner in rinse water. Rinse at pH 9–11 can irritate skin — wear gloves and goggles during maintenance. Wet floors are a slip hazard — use anti-slip mats. Drain water goes to the treatment system — never to direct drain.
