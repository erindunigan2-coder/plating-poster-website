Water Break Time
That’s how long a clean surface takes to water-break. If water sheets in a continuous film, the part is clean. If it beads up, it’s not.
Water Break Test

Key Procedures 7 steps

1
Rack parts securely. Verify electrical contact at every hook and tip — loose parts mean skip-plating later.
2
Confirm cleaner tank is at operating temperature (130–160°F) before loading.
3
Immerse rack fully. All surfaces must be submerged — trapped air pockets prevent cleaning.
4
Hold 3–10 min depending on soil load. Heavy draw compounds and heat-treat scale need the full soak.
5
Agitate parts by lifting the rack 2–3 times during the soak, or run mechanical/air agitation.
6
Remove rack and allow to drain 10–15 seconds over the cleaner tank.
7
Water break test: dip in clean water and observe. Water must sheet uniformly. Any beading = re-clean.

Do best practice

  • Run water break test on every load
  • Maintain concentration by titration
  • Skim floating oil from surface regularly
  • Match cleaner to soil type per vendor TDS

Don’t avoid

  • Assume a timed soak means the part is clean
  • Top off with water only — dilutes the cleaner
  • Let the oil layer build up — it redeposits
  • Use one cleaner for everything without checking TDS

Troubleshooting Common issues

Water Break Fails

Low cleaner concentration or temp too low. Titrate cleaner; increase to operating temp.

Oily Film After Cleaning

Floating oil layer redepositing. Install or clean surface skimmer; dump if heavily contaminated.

White Residue on Parts

Hard water salts or cleaner residue. Check rinse water quality; verify rinse is adequate.

Etching / Discoloration

Cleaner too aggressive; temp too high. Reduce temp; switch to milder formulation.

Spotty Adhesion Downstream

Parts not uniformly clean; air pockets during soak. Reposition on rack; add agitation.

Excessive Foam

Oil emulsification or contamination. Skim oil; use defoamer per TDS.

⚠ Safety — Hot Alkaline Solution
Chemical burns on contact. Steam and mist irritate airways. PPE: chemical splash goggles, face shield (for charging/dumping), elbow-length chemical gloves, apron, chemical-resistant boots. Skin contact: flush 15 min. Eye contact: eyewash 15 min. Spill: contain with absorbent; neutralize with citric acid if per site SOP.
