NO AIR DRY
Rinse and Move to the Second Zincate Immediately
The bare aluminum is completely unprotected right now — the zinc is gone and the oxide wants to come back. If parts dry in the air even for a few seconds, an invisible layer forms that ruins the second zinc coat. Rinse 30–60 seconds, then go straight to the second zincate. No waiting. No breaks.
What You Must Do
1
Let parts drain 10–15 seconds over the acid tank. This keeps acid out of the rinse water.
2
Submerge parts in the rinse tank. Move the rack gently for 30–60 seconds. Make sure water is flowing.
3
Transfer to the second zincate immediately. Do NOT let parts sit in air. Do NOT set them down. Go directly to the zincate tank.
✓ Right Way
  • Parts stay wet at all times
  • Moved to second zincate within 30 seconds
  • Rinse water flowing, not stagnant
  • No air drying between tanks
✗ Wrong Way
  • Parts sitting in air between tanks
  • Slow transfer — waiting for next rack
  • Stagnant rinse water (not flowing)
  • Setting parts down to take a break
Common Problems
What You SeeLikely CauseWhat To Do
Second zincate looks unevenParts dried between rinse and zincate, or acid carryoverMove faster. Rinse longer if needed. Tell your supervisor.
Parts look hazy or dull after rinseParts air dried — oxide formingDo not let parts sit in air. Go straight to the second zincate.
Plating peels later (copper strike onward)Air dry or acid carryover ruined the second zincateTell your supervisor. Parts need to go back to the etch step.
⚠ Safety
Rinse water contains residual nitric acid. Wear chemical-resistant gloves when handling parts. If rinse water gets on your skin, flush with water for 15 minutes. If it gets in your eyes, flush immediately and get medical help. Do not lean over the tank — acid fumes may be present.
Operator reference only. Follow your facility SOPs. Report any process deviations to your supervisor.
