Organic Dye Absorption Into Open Pores

Color lives in the pores. pH, temperature, and time control depth and durability.

pH 5.5
Target pH — The Color Sweet Spot
Organic dyes absorb best at pH 5.0–6.0. Too low: dye attacks oxide, colors fade. Too high: poor absorption, pale results. Use a calibrated pH meter. Dye uptake depends on pore size, temperature, and immersion time. Consistent results require all three to be controlled tightly.

Procedures 5 steps

1
Verify dye bath: pH (5.0–6.0), temperature (130–145°F), and dye concentration.
2
Transfer from rinse immediately. Every minute of delay reduces dye uptake.
3
Immerse and agitate gently. Hold for required time (5–30 min by color depth).
4
Check color against standard (wet), then rinse briefly in DI and move to seal.
5
Pull a test coupon before running production loads. Confirm color match against the standard — dye concentration drifts over time.

Do best

  • Monitor pH with calibrated meter every batch
  • Keep dye tanks covered when not in use
  • Use DI water for all dye makeup
  • Color-match wet against a sealed standard
  • Run a test coupon before each production batch

Don’t avoid

  • Mix different dye brands in the same tank
  • Let parts air-dry before dyeing
  • Use pH strips instead of a meter
  • Skip rinse between dye and seal
  • Mix dye brands or chemistries without supplier approval
⚠ Safety — Hot Dye Solution + pH Adjusters
Dye tanks operate at 130–145°F — burn risk from hot liquid and steam. NH₄OH releases ammonia fumes — use with ventilation. Dyes stain skin and clothing permanently. PPE: goggles, chemical gloves, apron. Ventilation required when adjusting pH. Dye solutions can stain skin and are difficult to remove. Wear chemical-resistant gloves and apron. Some dyes may cause sensitization with repeated exposure.
Note: This step applies to Type II decorative anodizing. Type III hard anodize bypasses dye and proceeds directly to seal.

Operator reference only. Verify all parameters against your chemistry supplier’s TDS and applicable specifications before production use.
