Decorative Chromium, Clear Seal, or Lacquer — Protecting the Nickel and Completing the System

Bright nickel alone tarnishes. What you put over it determines whether the finish lasts months or decades.

Decorative chromium is a thin, hard, blue-white layer over bright nickel that provides tarnish resistance, scratch resistance, and the characteristic chrome appearance. The nickel underneath does the heavy lifting for corrosion protection.

⚠ Safety — Stage 7
Hexavalent chromium (Cr⁶⁺) is a confirmed human carcinogen (IARC Group 1). OSHA PEL: 5 µg/m³. Full PPE required: supplied-air respirator or PAPR with P100/OV cartridge, full face shield, chemical suit, double gloves, chemical boots. Medical surveillance per OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1026. Chrome mist suppressant mandatory. Trivalent chrome is less toxic but still requires standard PPE and ventilation.
