30 sec
Maximum Activation Time for Carbon Steel
5% HCl dissolves the oxide layer without attacking base metal — but only within the time window. Under-activation leaves oxide that blocks plating adhesion; over-activation etches the substrate, roughens the surface, and loads the acid with dissolved iron that slows future etch rates. Proper acid activation creates an oxide-free, chemically active surface for optimal zinc-nickel adhesion. The transfer window between activation and immersion in the plating bath must be kept under 30 seconds to prevent flash reoxidation.

Key Procedures 7 steps

1
Verify HCl at 50 g/L (5% v/v) by titration before each shift. Low acid leaves oxide; high acid attacks base metal.
2
Confirm ambient temp 18–30°C — NEVER heat this tank. Elevated temperature accelerates hydrogen embrittlement risk.
3
Immerse mild steel 15–30 s, high-strength steel (>40 HRC) 15 s max to limit hydrogen absorption.
4
Observe fine uniform bubbling across the entire part surface — uneven bubbling indicates residual soils or passive areas.
5
Remove immediately; transfer to rinse within 30 s. Delay causes flash rust and adhesion failure downstream.
6
Rinse must flow >4 L/min with counter-current overflow to strip all residual acid from recesses and blind holes.
7
Monitor dissolved Fe weekly by titration — dump and remake when Fe exceeds 50 g/L (etch rate drops sharply).

Do best

  • Timer every immersion
  • Monitor Fe weekly by titration
  • Ensure flowing rinse after activation
  • Use inhibitor for high-strength steel
  • Transfer to plating within 30 seconds of activation

Don’t avoid

  • Heat the acid tank — ever
  • Use HF unless approved for stainless
  • Recycle acid indefinitely
  • Allow >30 s air exposure before rinse
  • Let activated parts sit in air — reoxidation causes adhesion failures

Troubleshooting

Skip Plating (Bare Spots)

Under-activation — oxide remains on the surface. Extend immersion 5–10 s; check acid concentration by titration; test for passivated or pre-plated steel.

Excessive Etching / Roughness

Over-activation or elevated Fe loading. Reduce immersion time; dump and remake acid when Fe >50 g/L. Compare Ra before and after activation.

Black Smut on Surface

Carbon smut from high-carbon steel or contaminated acid bath. Add wetting agent at supplier-recommended dosage; consider mechanical pre-clean or blast.

Adhesion Failure (Peel in Bend Test)

Oxide not fully removed or re-oxidized before plating due to slow transfer. Shorten transfer time to <30 s; confirm water-break test passes in rinse.

⚠ Safety — Acid Fumes + Hydrogen
HCl fumes are corrosive — PEL 5 ppm ceiling (OSHA). Continuous local exhaust ventilation required. H₂SO₄ causes severe burns — never add water to acid (exothermic). Hydrogen gas evolved during dissolution — explosive at 4–75% in air; ensure no ignition sources near tank. PPE: acid-rated splash goggles, face shield, acid-resistant gloves, apron. Emergency shower and eyewash within 10 s travel. Acid activation baths produce corrosive fumes. Ensure adequate ventilation over the tank. Wear acid-resistant gloves and splash goggles.
