Prepare the Surface So the Nickel Deposit Can Initiate

The part material determines the activation process. Steel is simplest. Aluminum needs a double zincate. Stainless steel needs a Wood’s Nickel Strike.

Aluminum Double Zincate
Aluminum’s tenacious oxide film prevents direct EN initiation. The double zincate process replaces the oxide with a thin, uniform zinc layer: desmut (dilute HNO₃) → 1st zincate → strip (dilute HNO₃) → 2nd zincate. The second immersion produces a finer, more uniform zinc film than the first — never skip it. Transfer to the EN bath within 30 seconds — the zinc layer oxidizes quickly in air.

⚠ Safety — Stage 3
Strong mineral acid — severe burns and eye damage. HCl produces fumes at room temperature — use local ventilation; never work without air extraction. HNO₃ (for aluminum desmut) produces yellow-brown NOx gas — if you see colored fumes, move away immediately. Goggles + face shield, acid-resistant gloves, apron, boots. Emergency shower within 10 seconds.
