Recovery Rinse First — Do Not Dump — Copper Has Value
Stage 1 is a stagnant recovery rinse. The concentrated copper dragout collected here is returned to the copper plate tank weekly — this recovers expensive copper cyanide chemistry. Stages 2–3 are counterflow rinses at ambient temperature. Minimum 60 seconds immersion per stage. All effluent routes to cyanide destruct — never to acid waste.

Recovery Rinse Economics
The first rinse tank captures the highest concentration of copper cyanide dragout. Copper cyanide is expensive and heavily regulated as a cyanide waste. By collecting this concentrated dragout and returning it to the plate tank weekly, you reduce both chemical costs and waste treatment volume. This single recovery tank can return 50–80% of dragged-out copper back to the process. Do not dilute the recovery rinse with flowing water — keep it stagnant to maintain concentration.

Effluent Routing

All rinse water from Stages 2–3 contains cyanide and must be routed to the cyanide destruct treatment system. Cyanide-bearing waste must never be mixed with acid waste streams — mixing cyanide with acid generates lethal HCN gas. Cyanide destruct typically uses alkaline chlorination or UV/peroxide oxidation to break down CN− before discharge. Verify your facility’s waste routing before operating this rinse station.

Effluent (Stages 2–3) → cyanide destruct. NEVER to acid waste drain.

Troubleshooting Multi-stage rinse issues

Recovery Rinse Overflowing

Excessive dragout volume or recovery tank too small. Ensure parts drain adequately over Cu plate tank before transfer. Check that counterflow water is not leaking into the recovery stage.

Parts Exiting with Residue

Insufficient rinse time or counterflow rate too low. Increase immersion time to 60+ seconds per stage. Check water flow rate in Stages 2–3. Verify counterflow plumbing is functioning correctly.

Silver Strike Adhesion Issues

Inadequate rinsing here allows copper plate dragout to contaminate the silver strike bath. Increase rinse time. Check final rinse water conductivity — should be low.

Cyanide Detected in Acid Waste

Plumbing error — cyanide rinse water routed to acid drain. STOP immediately. This is a safety emergency — acid + cyanide = HCN gas. Verify all drain routing. Correct plumbing before resuming operations.

⚠ Safety
All rinse stages contain cyanide from copper plate dragout. Treat every tank in this rinse system as a cyanide-containing solution. Wear chemical-resistant gloves and face shield. Never route cyanide rinse water to acid waste drains — mixing cyanide with acid generates lethal HCN gas. All effluent must go to the cyanide destruct treatment system. Maintain ventilation. If you detect a bitter almond odor or HCN monitor alarms, evacuate the area immediately.
