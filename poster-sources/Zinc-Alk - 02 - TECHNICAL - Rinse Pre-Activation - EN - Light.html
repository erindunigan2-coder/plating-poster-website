RINSE

Strip the Cleaner Film Before the Acid Dip

A rinse isn’t a rest stop. It’s a checkpoint.

PP
Plating Posters

<500
µS/cm — Rinse Conductivity
Above this, you’re dragging alkaline cleaner into the acid activation tank. That wastes acid, raises pH, and reduces activation effectiveness.

Key Procedures 7 steps

1
Transfer from cleaner immediately — do not allow parts to air-dry.
2
Immerse fully. Agitate rack up and down 3–5 times to break the boundary layer.
3
Hold 30–60 seconds minimum.
4
Counter-current systems: parts enter the “dirty” stage first, exit through the “clean” stage.
5
Check conductivity daily. If >500 µS/cm, increase water flow or dump and refill.
6
Drain 10–15 seconds before moving to the acid activation tank.
7
Visual inspect: parts should look uniformly wet with no soapy film or bubbles.

Do best practice

  • Agitate the rack — passive dipping is not rinsing
  • Monitor conductivity and log it every shift
  • Drain adequately before moving to the acid dip
  • Keep the rinse tank clean — no debris or floating oil

Don’t avoid

  • Just dunk and pull — the cleaner film stays on
  • Assume the overflow is handling it
  • Rush the transfer — drag-in neutralizes the acid
  • Ignore scum on the rinse surface

Troubleshooting Common issues

Conductivity Stays High

Cleaner drag-out excessive or flow rate too low. Increase drain time from cleaner; increase rinse flow; add counter-current stage.

Soapy Residue on Parts

Inadequate rinse time or agitation. Extend dwell; add manual agitation; check cleaner for excess surfactant.

Acid Dip Goes Alkaline

Excessive cleaner carry-over. Improve rinse efficiency; check conductivity; add a second rinse stage.

Oil on Rinse Surface

Cleaner tank contaminated; oil carry-over. Clean cleaner tank skimmer; skim rinse tank.

Parts Dry Before Acid

Transfer too slow; line spacing too wide. Speed up transfer; mist spray in transit if needed.

⚠ Safety
Residual alkaline cleaner in the rinse water. Mild skin irritation risk. Slippery floor around rinse tanks. PPE: chemical splash goggles, chemical-resistant gloves, non-slip footwear. Eye contact: flush at eyewash 15 min.
