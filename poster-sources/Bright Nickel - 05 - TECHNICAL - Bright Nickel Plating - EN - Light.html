⚠ Safety — Bright Nickel Plating
Hot acidic nickel solution (pH 3.8–4.2, 130–150°F) — causes burns and is a dermal sensitizer. Nickel compounds are classified as known carcinogens by inhalation (Category 1A, GHS) and skin sensitizers. Chronic exposure causes allergic contact dermatitis (nickel itch). Air agitation generates nickel mist — local exhaust ventilation mandatory. Rectifier = high amperage — LOTO for all maintenance. OSHA PEL: 1.0 mg/m³ (insoluble Ni); ACGIH TLV: 0.1 mg/m³ (soluble Ni — applicable to this bath). Use PP/PVC tanks.
