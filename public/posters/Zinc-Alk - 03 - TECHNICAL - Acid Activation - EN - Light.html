Dip Time
The acid dissolves surface oxides and produces a chemically active steel surface. Over-dip and you etch the base metal. Under-dip and oxides remain. Time it.

Key Procedures 7 steps

1
Transfer from rinse immediately. Surfaces must be wet.
2
Immerse fully. Tilt complex parts to displace trapped air.
3
Hold 15–60 sec. Heavy oxide/cast iron: longer end. Clean low-carbon steel: 15–30 sec.
4
Observe gas evolution — light, uniform bubbling = working. Violent bubbling = too concentrated or too long.
5
Remove promptly at end of timed dip. Do not leave parts sitting in acid.
6
Drain 10–15 sec over the acid tank.
7
Transfer to pre-plate rinse immediately. Do not let activated surfaces dry or re-oxidize.

Do best practice

  • Time every dip with a timer, not your judgment
  • Move parts to rinse immediately after activation
  • Monitor acid strength and iron buildup weekly
  • Use inhibitor for high-strength or spring steel

Don’t avoid

  • “Eyeball” the dip time — over-etching causes pitting
  • Let activated parts sit in air — oxide reforms in seconds
  • Run the acid until it stops working — that wastes parts
  • Dip >40 HRC steel in uninhibited acid without review

Troubleshooting Common issues

Dark Oxide Remains

Acid too dilute or time too short. Increase concentration; extend time (max 60 sec); consider pre-pickle for heavy scale.

Pitting / Etching

Acid too concentrated, time too long, or acid too warm. Reduce concentration; shorten dip; add inhibitor.

Adhesion Failure Downstream

Incomplete activation — oxide remains. Re-evaluate acid strength and dip time; check cleaner drag-in raising pH.

Excessive HCl Fuming

High concentration or poor ventilation. Reduce to minimum effective; check exhaust system; consider H&sub2;SO&sub4; alternative.

Dark Smut on Parts

High dissolved iron or copper contamination. Dump and recharge acid; check incoming parts for Cu alloy.

⚠ Safety — Strong Mineral Acid
HCl releases corrosive fumes at room temperature. H&sub2;SO&sub4; causes severe dehydration burns. H&sub2; gas evolved during pickling is flammable. PPE: goggles + face shield, acid-resistant gloves (butyl/neoprene), apron, boots, respirator if ventilation is inadequate. Skin: flush 15 min. Eyes: eyewash 15 min. HCl inhalation: fresh air immediately. Spill: neutralize with soda ash. Do NOT use water alone on concentrated H&sub2;SO&sub4;.
