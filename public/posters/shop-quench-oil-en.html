Diffusion & Heat — Oil Quench
OIL QUENCH
Rapid cooling in quench oil for hardening
Oil quenching cools parts fast enough to form martensite without the thermal shock of water — oil temperature and agitation must be controlled to prevent soft spots and distortion.

THE KEY NUMBER
50–80 °C (120–175 °F) oil temperature
Quench oil must be pre-heated to the target range — cold oil quenches too fast (cracking risk), hot oil quenches too slow (soft parts).
WHAT YOU MUST DO
1
Check oil temperature before quenching — it must be within the specified range
2
Verify the oil level covers all parts by at least 6 inches — low oil level causes uneven cooling
3
Agitate the oil or move the rack during the quench — stagnant oil creates a vapor blanket that slows cooling
4
Immerse parts quickly and fully — do not lower slowly, as this causes differential cooling and distortion
5
After quenching, drain parts over the tank and transfer to wash — oil left on parts contaminates downstream processes
COMMON PROBLEMS
Problem What to Look For What to Do
Soft spotsLocalized areas read below hardness spec — often on surfaces that faced each other in the loadImprove agitation and part spacing — vapor blanket is not being broken in those areas
CrackingCracks visible after quench, typically at sharp corners, holes, or section changesOil may be too cold or agitation too aggressive — raise oil temperature and reduce agitation; temper immediately
Fire / smokingFlames or heavy smoke when hot parts enter the oilOil temperature may be near flash point or oil level is too low — check temperature, top off oil, and verify fire suppression is ready
⚠ SAFETY
●Quench oil fires are a serious risk — know the location and operation of the fire suppression system before every shift
●Never add water to hot quench oil — water turns to steam instantly and causes a violent eruption
●Oil mist and smoke are respiratory irritants — ensure exhaust ventilation is operating above the quench tank
●Oil on floors is a slip and fire hazard — clean spills immediately and keep the area around the quench tank clear
Consult the supervisor before adjusting process parameters.
