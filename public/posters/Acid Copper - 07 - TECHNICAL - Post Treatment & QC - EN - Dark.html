Roles — What Copper Does Next
Acid copper is usually an undercoat (goes on to bright nickel + chrome in the decorative triad), a functional / PCB deposit (verified and finished), or a decorative bare-copper / antique finish (which must be protected because copper tarnishes fast). Know which role applies before you leave this stage.

H-Embrittlement Bake Decision Point
For hardened steel substrates (>40 HRC): bake within 4 hours of the last electrodeposition step at 375 ±25°F for a minimum of 8 hours per ASTM B850. Verify customer spec for whether the bake occurs before subsequent nickel/chrome. The 4-hour clock starts at completion of the last electrodeposition step.

Final Rinse & Dry Addendum — Stage 8

DI water rinse, ambient, gentle immersion 15–30 s · warm air dry 120–140°F max · keep bright copper protected with anti-tarnish or an immediate topcoat if the finish is bare copper · handle only with clean, dry lint-free gloves — fingerprints etch permanently into fresh copper.

Troubleshooting handoff & finish issues

Copper Tarnishes / Darkens

Cause: Expected — copper oxidizes fast. Fix: Apply anti-tarnish / lacquer; reduce exposure.

Nickel Haze Over Copper

Cause: Copper oxidized before nickel; slow transfer. Fix: Activate copper; speed transfer; keep wet.

Nickel Peels From Copper

Cause: Passive / oxidized copper surface. Fix: Acid-activate copper before nickel.

Fingerprints Etched in Copper

Cause: Bare-handed handling of fresh copper. Fix: Clean gloves only.

Poor Solderability (PCB)

Cause: Oxidation; organic residue; wrong finish. Fix: Verify finish / OSP; clean; re-test.

Copper Climbing in Nickel Bath

Cause: Copper drag-in on the handoff. Fix: Tighten post-plate rinse; add recovery + second rinse.

⚠ Safety Summary — Depends on Path
Anti-tarnish / blackening chemistries vary — some contain selenium or sulfide compounds; consult SDS before use. If a hexavalent chromate anti-tarnish is used, Cr⁶ / Cr(VI) is a confirmed carcinogen (OSHA PEL 5 µg/m³ [VERIFY]) — full respiratory protection and engineering controls apply. Lacquer solvents — flammable; ventilation required, no ignition sources. Standard chemical PPE (goggles, gloves, apron) throughout; current SDS accessible at every station.

Technical reference only. Verify all parameters against your chemistry supplier’s TDS, customer specifications, and applicable regulatory requirements before production use. Anti-tarnish, blackening, and hexavalent chromate chemistries carry distinct hazards; copper tarnishes rapidly and hardened-steel substrates may require post-plate baking — always consult current SDS and customer specs.
