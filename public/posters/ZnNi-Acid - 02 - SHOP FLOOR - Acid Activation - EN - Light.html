30 sec
Maximum Activation Time — Carbon Steel
5% HCl dissolves the oxide layer without attacking base metal — but only within the time window. Over-activation etches the substrate, roughens the surface, and loads the acid with dissolved iron that drags into the Zn-Ni plating bath. Acid activation removes the passive oxide film so zinc-nickel can bond directly to the base metal. This must be done immediately before plating.
Key Procedures
1
Verify HCl at 50 g/L (5% v/v) by titration before each shift.
2
Confirm tank temp 18–30°C — NEVER heat this tank.
3
Immerse mild steel 15–45 s, high-strength steel 10–20 s max.
4
Watch for fine uniform bubbling — that means oxide is dissolving.
5
Remove immediately; transfer to rinse within 30 s — dissolved Fe drags into the Zn-Ni bath.
6
Rinse flow >4 L/min — complete acid and Fe removal before plating.
7
Monitor dissolved Fe weekly — dump at 50 g/L; keep <30 g/L.

Do best

  • Use a timer every immersion
  • Check dissolved Fe weekly by titration
  • Ensure flowing rinse after activation
  • Use inhibitor for high-strength steel
  • Transfer to plating within 30 seconds of activation

Don’t avoid

  • Heat the acid tank — ever
  • Recycle acid indefinitely — Fe ruins plating
  • Allow >30 s air exposure before rinse
  • Skip the timer — over-activation causes etching
  • Let activated parts sit in air — reoxidation begins immediately
⚠ Safety — Acid Fumes + Hydrogen
HCl fumes are corrosive — PEL 5 ppm ceiling (OSHA). Local exhaust ventilation required at all times. Hydrogen gas is evolved during dissolution — explosive at 4–75% in air; no ignition sources near tank. PPE: acid-rated splash goggles, face shield, acid-resistant gloves, apron. Emergency shower and eyewash within 10 s travel. Hydrochloric acid produces corrosive fumes. Ensure adequate tank ventilation. Wear acid-resistant gloves and splash goggles at all times near the acid tank.
