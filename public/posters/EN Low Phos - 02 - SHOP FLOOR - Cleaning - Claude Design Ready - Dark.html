CLEANING

Stage 1 of 7 — Alkaline tank cleaning

Electroless plating only deposits on a completely clean surface. Contamination doesn't reduce quality — it stops it entirely.

The Key Number

The Key Number
60–80 °C
Cleaner bath temperature. Below 60 °C the cleaner does not work well.

What You Must Do

1

Verify the bath is between 60 and 80 °C before submerging parts.

2

Submerge parts completely — trapped air pockets hold grease and cause unplated areas.

3

Keep parts in the tank 3 to 10 minutes with agitation.

4

Run the water break test: water must sheet off in a continuous film. If it beads up, clean again.

5

Transfer parts to the rinse immediately — do not let them air-dry.

Common Problems

ProblemWhat to Look ForWhat to Do
Failed water breakWater beads up when rinsingIncrease time or temperature; replenish cleaner
Unplated areasBare spots in the final depositRe-clean; verify water break before continuing
Rough depositIrregular nickel surfaceFilter the cleaner bath and check for particulate. If the problem persists, the EN bath may also need filtration — notify the supervisor.

Safety — This Stage

●Wear splash goggles, nitrile or neoprene gloves, and chemical-resistant apron.
●Alkaline cleaners cause serious burns — the risk increases at operating temperature (60–80 °C).
●When preparing or replenishing the cleaner, always add chemical to water — never water to concentrated chemical.
●Eyewash station must be within 10 seconds of the tank.

This poster is a process guide — for adjustments, always consult the supervisor or chemistry technician. Reference data shown is typical; verify against your supplier's TDS before production.
