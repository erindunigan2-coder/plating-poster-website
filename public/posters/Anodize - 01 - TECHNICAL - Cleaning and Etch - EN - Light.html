Two Stages — Clean Then Etch
Soak clean removes oils and shop soils without attacking the substrate. Alkaline etch (NaOH) is a controlled chemical attack that removes the natural oxide and produces a uniform matte finish. They are sequential — never skip the clean. The etch rate depends on alloy, temperature, NaOH concentration, and dissolved aluminum. Always run coupons when changing alloys or after bath maintenance.

Troubleshooting

Uneven Etch / Streaking

Incomplete cleaning — oil or fingerprints block etch. Extend clean time; check cleaner concentration. Always rinse between clean and etch.

Excessive Metal Loss

Etch temp or time too high. Reduce to minimum needed for matte finish. Critical on thin-wall parts.

Heavy Smut After Etch

Normal for high-Si or high-Cu alloys — the desmut step (03) removes it. If excessive, check alloy segregation or etch concentration.

Shiny Spots (No Etch)

Residual oil, tape adhesive, or handling marks. Improve pre-clean or add ultrasonic agitation.

Etch Rate Dropping

Dissolved Al above 50 g/L. Titrate Al content; decant or replace bath. High Al slows the reaction and produces inconsistent finish.

Foam / Bubbling in Cleaner

Normal with surfactant cleaners. Excessive foam may indicate drag-in contamination or wrong product concentration.

Do / Don’t

Do best

  • Rinse between soak clean and etch
  • Use non-silicated cleaner for anodize lines
  • Track dissolved Al in etch bath
  • Wear full PPE at etch tank (NaOH burns)
  • Check cleaner concentration by titration at start of each shift

Don’t avoid

  • Skip soak clean and go straight to etch
  • Use silicated cleaners (inhibits etch uniformity)
  • Let parts dry between tanks
  • Etch 2xxx alloys at high temp without testing
  • Neutralize caustic burns with acid — flush with water only for 15+ minutes
⚠ Safety — Hot Caustic (NaOH)
Sodium hydroxide causes severe chemical burns on contact. Etch tank operates at 122–160°F — splashes are immediate burns. Reaction with aluminum generates hydrogen gas (H&sub2;) — flammable in confined spaces. PPE: splash goggles + face shield, elbow-length chemical gloves, apron, chemical boots. Continuous ventilation required over etch tank. Eyewash and safety shower within 10 seconds of walking distance. If caustic splashes on skin, flush immediately with water for at least 15 minutes. Do not apply vinegar or acid — this worsens the burn. Hydrogen gas is generated during the etch reaction — ensure adequate ventilation above etch tanks. No ignition sources within 10 feet of the etch station.
