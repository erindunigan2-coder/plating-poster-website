⚠ Line Safety Overview
NaOH (caustic soda) in cleaning stages — severe chemical burns. HCl in acid activation — ventilate at >1 m/s face velocity. Nickel compounds are Category 1A carcinogens by inhalation — airborne Ni must stay <0.05 mg/m³. Chloride mist from the acid bath — corrosive to equipment and lungs. H₂ gas at cathode — explosive at 4–75% LEL. HE bake mandatory for high-strength steel (>39 HRC) within 4 hours of plating. PPE required at every tank. Report any chemical spills, unusual odors, or equipment malfunctions to your supervisor immediately. Never work alone on the line during startup or shutdown.
