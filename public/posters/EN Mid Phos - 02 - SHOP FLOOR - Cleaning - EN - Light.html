60–80°C
Cleaner Bath Temperature
Below 60°C the cleaner does not emulsify oils effectively. Most alkaline soak cleaners perform best at 70–80°C. Heavy machining oil or buffing compound may require 8–10 minutes of soak time.

✓ Do
  • Water-break test every rack — no beading allowed
  • Submerge parts completely — no air pockets
  • Transfer to rinse immediately — do not air-dry
  • Use agitation to reach recesses
✗ Don’t
  • Skip cleaning — EN only plates on clean surfaces
  • Use too-hot cleaner on aluminum (<140°F for Al)
  • Touch cleaned parts with bare hands
  • Let parts dry between cleaning and rinse

⚠ Safety — Stage 1
Alkaline cleaner at 60–80°C — causes chemical burns. pH 12–14. Goggles, nitrile or neoprene gloves, chemical-resistant apron. When preparing cleaner, always add chemical to water — never water to concentrated chemical. Eyewash station within 10 seconds of the tank. Skin contact: flush 15 min.
Operator reference only. Follow your facility SOPs. Report any process deviations to your supervisor.
